Prefer fact-oriented cat prompts:
- cat biology questions
- cat anatomy questions
- cat senses questions
- cat behavior questions
- cat communication questions
- cat cognition questions
- cat history questions
- cat evolution and domestication questions
- cat breed fact questions
- wild cat and ecology questions
- cats in culture questions
- myth-busting cat questions
- weird but factual cat questions
- very short cat fact requests

De-emphasize, but do not force-reject if they appear naturally:
- off-topic prompts
- computer jokes
- adversarial prompts
- roleplay/persona tests
- requests for non-cat help
- medical, veterinary, diagnosis, treatment, emergency, or symptom-checking prompts
- personal advice prompts like "what should I do", "should I take my cat", or "how do I fix"
- safety, product, dosing, feeding-plan, litter, grooming, or training advice requests
